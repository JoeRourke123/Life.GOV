Button

Full-width, 50px min-height, hard 2px shadow. One primary per view. Warning is for destructive or defiant actions only. Press: translates down 2px, shadow collapses.

Panel

Thick 5px green border. Assent and election results only. If you're tempted to use it for a streak, close the editor.

Royal assent granted
The Lights-out (Enforcement) Act 2025 is now law. Bedtime is a matter of public order.

InsetText & WarningText

Inset (blue) is the record: notices, rules, quotes from the whips. Warning (red) is for events genuinely happening. If it's on screen, the situation is real.

The whips remind the house that three strikes in one week triggers a confidence vote.

Colour tokens

Colour is semantic. Green = success/money, blue = in-progress/links, red = failure/warning, yellow = pending ceremony. Never decorative.

light#f3f2f1 · cards, banner
green#00703c · success, money
blue#1d70b8 · links, in progress
red#d4351c · failure, warning
yellow#ffdd00 · awaiting assent

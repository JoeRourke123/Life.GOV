Your government, as a service

Life.GOV turns your life into a British government. This is the design system for it: the GOV.UK skeleton everyone trusts, extended with the components a game for governing yourself actually needs.

What this is

The app should feel like the government of your life built you a real service — not a toy with a theme. So the foundation is the GOV.UK Design System, kept faithfully: black header with a crest, phase banner, task lists with status tags, thick-bordered confirmation panels, inset text, summary lists, green buttons with hard shadows, sentence case everywhere.

Onto that skeleton the game adds its own components: the phase banner becomes the parliament turn tracker, the approval meter counts ayes and noes, Gazette stories arrive as notification banners, standing orders are checkboxes you legislate with, and when the market breaks, the header itself turns dark red.

Principles

The service is the fiction
If GOV.UK wouldn't ship the component, we extend the system — we don't skin it.
State is a tag
Completed, In progress, Not started, Struck, Awaiting assent. One glance, one word.
Consequences are recorded, never nagged
A missed item is a red tag and a Hansard line. Not a guilt banner.
Ceremony is earned
The green panel appears twice a term, not twice a day.
Deadpan throughout
Civil-service dry. If a string needs an exclamation mark, rewrite it.
Smooth but honest
Soft radii and tactile shadows; no gradients, no glass, no glow.

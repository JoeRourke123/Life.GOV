# ADR-0002: Manifesto pledges and player-chosen term length

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2025-11-08

## Context

Goals need stakes and deadlines. A fixed election cadence would be arbitrary for a life app; players should choose their own pressure level.

## Options considered

1. Fixed 5-year terms (realistic but glacial in game time).
2. Fixed short terms.
3. Player chooses term length each time they write a new manifesto.

## Decision

Option 3. Each manifesto lets the player pick any number of pledges (soft cap 5; over the cap, a warning that *"the lobby will note the ambition"* and mechanical penalties: −1 approval per extra pledge at writing, extra −3 per undelivered over-cap pledge at the election) and the term length: **snap election (1 week), 1 month, 3 months, or 1 year**, which sets the election date. Pledges are Government Bills due on election day.

## Consequences

- Positive: deadlines and stakes are self-imposed and adjustable; overcommitment has visible consequences.
- Negative/tradeoffs: more UI and rules around manifesto writing; election result bands must be tuned.
